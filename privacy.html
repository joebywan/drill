Drill and your data

Last changed 30 October 2026.

The short version

Drill keeps your progress on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you turn on sync. There are no accounts, emails, passwords, ads or trackers.

If you turn on sync

Sync lets you use Drill on more than one device and swap cards with friends. When it's on, Drill sends two things to its own server at api.knowhowit.com.au:

  • Your progress, locked. Your device locks it (AES-GCM encryption) with a key made from your recovery code before it leaves. The server stores it but can't read it. That includes anything you write in Reflect.
  • A small card for friends: a name and animal picked from a list, your streak, how many lessons you've done and which unit you're on. Only friends you've accepted can see it.

The server also keeps your friend code, who your friends are, any cheers from the last month (picked from a fixed list), and, for a few minutes, a pairing code you asked for. To stop misuse it counts requests per network address for a short time; it doesn't keep a log of them.

The server runs on Cloudflare (Workers and D1). Nothing is sold or shared with anyone else, and nothing is used for advertising.

Your recovery code

Your recovery code is the key to your synced progress. It stays on your devices; Drill's server never sees it. Anyone who has it can see your progress, so keep it to yourself. If no device has it, nobody (including us) can unlock your synced progress.

Children

Drill doesn't ask for a name, email, age or location, so it's suitable for people of any age.

Your choices

  • See or keep a copy: in Drill, ⋯ then "Save to a file". That file is everything Drill holds about you.
  • Delete: in Drill, ⋯ then "Delete my data". It removes everything on the server straight away. Progress on your device stays until you clear it.
  • Delete without the app: use your recovery code below.

Delete with a recovery code

This deletes everything Drill's server keeps for that recovery code. It can't be undone.
